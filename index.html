How do I unlock Worlds 2 to 6?

World 1 is free. Tap Unlock full game on the main menu, or Unlock Worlds 2-6 after beating World 1, to buy the full adventure. It's a one-time purchase, and it's yours to keep.

I already bought the game. How do I get it back?

On a new iPhone, or after reinstalling, tap Restore purchases on the main menu. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is saved on your iPhone. Deleting the app erases progress, but your purchase can always be restored.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
